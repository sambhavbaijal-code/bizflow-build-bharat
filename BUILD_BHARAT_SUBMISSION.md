# BharatCode Build League submission draft

**Project name:** BizFlow

**Short description:** BizFlow is a practical multi-business ERP for small shop owners. One login manages separate workspaces for multiple firms, with product catalogues, stock, barcode lookup, customers, purchases, and sales. It calculates GST in the app and shows the tax breakdown on invoices. An optional BharatCode assistant answers operational questions from a limited stock and sales summary, only after the owner approves sharing. Customer names and invoice-level details are excluded from AI requests.
